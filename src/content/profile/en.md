I'm a PhD student (2024–2027) working on how neural networks can serve as surrogate models for massively parallel simulations, with a particular focus on how a trained model is deployed on a cluster. The work sits in AI for Science, at the intersection of deep learning and high-performance computing, and currently targets computational fluid dynamics (CFD). I'm advised by Bruno Raffin, Jean-Marc Gratien, and Raphaël Gayno.
